The model

how a pick is actually made now

v7 replaces the old qualitative checklist with a transparent quantitative core. Every pick starts from a Meros Power Rating (an ELO-style class rating updated each game by margin versus expectation), then blends form, venue, availability, rest and a market anchor into a single calibrated win probability. Margin and tier are derived from that probability, not from gut.

∑ Win-probability weighting

Probability is passed through a logistic curve and clamped to a 1–99% range. Tiers: STRONG ≥68%, LEAN 57–68%, COIN FLIP 50–57%. No LOCKs. Ever.

⌥ Margin & bet discipline
①
Favourite margin gateCramped prices cap the margin. Under $1.30 you cannot tip more than ~12, regardless of class. Kills the Broncos −20 @ $1.11 mistake.
②
Coin-flip discipline50–57% games get a margin of 1–4. Protects the Superbru margin bonus, never reaches.
③
Divergence-only Bold PlayA Bold Play fires only when model probability beats market implied by ≥10 points with a named cause. Otherwise none. Discipline over theatre.

Recalibration

what broke, and what v7 changed

An independent pass over R5–R14 found the cold streak wasn't bad luck, it was three repeating faults. Each one is now a coded rule, not a lesson we hope to remember.

✕ The faults
1
Bold Play decay6 straight landed (R6–R11), then 3 straight missed: Souths, Warriors, Raiders 0–26. All the same shape — backing a hot-form dog against a clearly better side.
2
Home-dog blind spotR13: three home underdogs won outright (Sharks, Tigers, Dragons). The model treated favourite prices as near-certainties.
3
Margin greed on short pricesBroncos −20 @ $1.11 against a desperate Dragons side, who then won. High variance, negative value.
4
"Form beats reputation" over-appliedA real rule, pushed too far. Class and depth set a floor that pure form can't override.
✓ The fixes (v7)
✓
Class floor via Meros Power RatingsTalent differential now anchors every pick at 40% weight. Form can lean a game, not flip it.
✓
Home-dog value flagLive underdogs at home with a positive model edge are surfaced, not buried under the market line.
✓
Favourite margin gateMargin is now a function of price. Short favourites can't be over-tipped.
✓
Divergence-only Bold Play + Origin exposureNo forced contrarian calls. Origin call-up asymmetry is now an explicit variable.
